To Sarah, on Girlfriend's Day 2026

My love,

You are not only the person I miss. You are the person who makes the waiting worthwhile. You have become part of how I experience every day. You are the first person I want to tell, the voice I want to hear, and the future my mind reaches for without being asked.

Loving you from this far away has taught me that closeness is not measured only in kilometres. It lives in being known, in being chosen, and in continuing to show up for one another even when showing up has to happen through a screen.

I hope you never wonder whether you are loved deeply. You are. In the exciting moments, in the ordinary ones, and even in the difficult ones. Not as an idea, not as a perfect version of you, but exactly as you are.

And if life somehow reset, if the calls, photographs, jokes, and memories disappeared, I believe I would still be drawn toward you.

I would find you again.I would choose you again.And I would fall in love with you again.

Always yours,

Zeid
